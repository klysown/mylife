Explore your Earth

Rotate a 3D globe, select routes, filter by year or transport mode, and switch to Apple Maps for city detail.

Keep a travel logbook

Group routes into journeys. Save dates, stops, operators, prices, notes, companions, and selected photos.

See your footprint

Review route distance estimates, recorded countries, transport modes, yearly activity, and spending grouped by currency.

Share a globe story

Create an eight-second portrait video with your routes and an optional name. Preview it, share the file, or save it to Photos.

Your archive, under your control

Export a backup with your photos and route geometry. Import a backup or your own GPX track. MyMundus has no developer-operated account service, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

The globe and saved records work offline. Place search, map imagery, and suggested road or walking routes require internet access. Flight arcs and distances are illustrative estimates; MyMundus is a travel journal, not a navigation or live flight-tracking service.
